Cat Hero Animal Soldiers

A mobile space shooter by LingTing Studio for Android and iOS.

Fly a cat hero's saucer to monster planets and blast your way through their shells, plate by plate, down to the glowing energy core. Clear the guards, beat the boss and blow up the core. Upgrade six weapons, unlock new spaceships, spin the prize wheel and let the autopilot dodge the bomb packs.

On iOS your progress and purchases are saved to iCloud, so they come back if you reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone or iPad.
