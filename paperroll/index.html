Paper Roll: Lazy Cat

A cozy puzzle game by LingTing Studio for Android and iOS.

Unroll, cut and match paper rolls in 10 relaxing puzzle modes in a cartoon bathroom, while a sleepy cat naps nearby. Earn paper rolls, spin the daily lucky wheel and pick up helper items in the shop.

Your progress and purchases can be saved to your Google Play Games account (Android) or iCloud (iOS), so they come back if you reinstall the game or move to a new device.
