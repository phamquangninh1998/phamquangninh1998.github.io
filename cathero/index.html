Cat Hero Spacecraft

A mobile space shooter by LingTing Studio for Android and iOS.

Fly a cat hero's spaceship around tiny planets, blast the monsters that invade them and rebuild the towns they wrecked. Upgrade your guns, unlock new spaceships with medals or wrenches, spin the lucky slot and save planet after planet.

Your progress and purchases can be saved to your Google Play Games account (Android) or iCloud (iOS), so they come back if you reinstall the game or move to a new device.
