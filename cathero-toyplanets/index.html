Cat Hero Toy Planets

A casual space shooter by LingTing Studio for Android and iOS.

Fly the cat's saucer around 100 giant toy planets (a donut, a Rubik cube, a teddy bear, a gold bar and many more), break them shell by shell, beat the boss and blow up the energy core. Upgrade six weapons, unlock new ships, spin the lucky wheel and play the pet slot machine.

On iOS your progress and purchases are saved to iCloud, so they come back if you reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone or iPad.
