Cat Hero Solo Play

A third-person mobile action game by LingTing Studio for Android and iOS.

A cat warrior slides down a rope from his saucer onto a tiny monster-filled planet and fights his way through the streets to save the town. He finds the monsters by himself: you pick the right weapon, call a helper cat and order bomb runs from your saucer. Win medals, collect ten suits of armor, upgrade six weapons and spin the prize wheel.

On iOS your progress and purchases are saved to iCloud, so they come back if you reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone or iPad.
