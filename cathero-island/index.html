Cat Hero Island

A cozy merge game by LingTing Studio for Android and iOS.

Help a crew of rescued cats turn an old garage into a spaceship workshop. Merge parts and snacks on the board, fill the cats' orders, build and launch eight ships, and relax with Snack Sort and Bolt Sort puzzles between shifts.
