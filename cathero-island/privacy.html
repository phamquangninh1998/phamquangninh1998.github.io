Cat Hero Island Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Cat Hero Island ("the game"), made by LingTing Studio ("we"), uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and we do not run our own servers that receive information about you.

Game progress on your device

The game stores your progress and settings on your device, for example coins, gems, energy, board items, ships, cats and options such as sound and vibration.

Update check

To tell you when a new version is out, the game asks Google Play (on Android) or the public App Store lookup service (on iOS) which version is the latest. Only the game's own app id is sent with that request.

In-app purchases

This version of the game sells nothing. If purchases are added later, they will be processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and we will never receive your payment card or billing details. This policy will be updated first.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional reward videos you choose to watch. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from the IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

  • Data on your device: uninstall the game, or use Settings > Reset progress in the game. All progress and settings stored on the device are deleted.
  • Need help? Email phamquangninh1998@gmail.com with the subject "Cat Hero data deletion". We reply within 30 days. We hold no other copy of your data.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
