100 Sports Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the game 100 Sports: Run Race ("the game"), made by LingTing Studio ("we"), uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your email address, phone number or location, and we do not run our own servers that receive information about you. If you play with friends, your player name and some game data are sent as described under "Play with friends".

Game progress on your device

The game stores your progress and settings on your device, for example coins, gems, owned skins, power-ups, medals, purchases and best distance.

Cloud save

To keep your progress and purchases when you reinstall the game or change device, the game saves a copy of that progress data:

  • on Android, in your Google Play Games account using Google Play Games Services (Saved Games). Signing in to Play Games is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy;
  • on iOS, in your iCloud account using iCloud key-value storage, handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

The saved copy contains only game progress. We cannot see your Google or Apple account details.

Play with friends

When you play with other people, the game uses Photon, a multiplayer service by Exit Games, to connect you. Your player name, avatar, room code, and your position and actions in the race are sent through Photon and shown to the other players in your room. Your player name comes from your Google Play Games or Game Center account. Photon receives your IP address to make the connection. This data is used only to run the race, and we do not store it. See the Photon Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment card or billing details. The game receives a purchase receipt to deliver the item and to restore it later.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from the IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. Buying "No Ads" removes the ads shown between zones.

If you are in a region where the law requires your consent for personalized ads, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice before ads load. You can change it at any time with the PRIVACY button on the home screen.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

To delete your 100 Sports: Run Race data:

  1. Data on your device: uninstall the game. All progress, settings and purchase records stored on the device are deleted at once.
  2. Cloud save on Android: open the Google Play Games app or your Google Account, go to Play Games settings, find 100 Sports and choose to delete the game's data. This deletes the saved progress copy immediately.
  3. Cloud save on iOS: open Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage, find 100 Sports and delete its data.
  4. Need help? Email phamquangninh1998@gmail.com with the subject "100 Sports data deletion". We reply within 30 days with the steps for your device. We hold no other copy of your data. Data sent through Photon during a race is not stored by us; for anything Photon keeps, see its privacy policy.

Deleting the cloud save removes coins, gems, skins, power-ups, medals, best distance and the record of items bought. "No Ads" can be restored later with Restore Purchases, because Google Play and the App Store keep their own purchase history under their own retention rules. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
