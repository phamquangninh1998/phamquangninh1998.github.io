100 Sports: Run Race

A mobile runner game by LingTing Studio for Android and iOS.

Run, ride, swim and fly through a long list of sports. Each mode has its own course, vehicles, stunts and rivals. Collect coins and gems, open chests, unlock skins and power-ups, and beat your best distance.

Your progress and purchases can be saved to your Google Play Games account (Android) or iCloud (iOS), so they come back if you reinstall the game or move to a new device.
